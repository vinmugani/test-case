FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a hub?

No. Hearth talks directly to Wi-Fi and Matter devices. For Zigbee or Z-Wave gear, any compatible bridge you already own works fine.

How much does Hearth cost?

The core app is free for unlimited devices. Hearth Plus adds extended camera history and advanced automations.

Can my whole family use it?

Yes. Invite as many household members as you like and choose what each person can see and control.

What happens if my internet goes down?

Local-first control means devices on your home network keep responding. Remote access resumes automatically once you're back online.
